Course Description:  
This is an introductory and specialization course which leads to  an Animation  National Certificate Level II (NC II). It covers Personal Entrepreneurial Competencies 
(PECs) ; Environment and Market ; five (5) Common Competencies ; and one (1) Core C ompetency that a high school  student ought to possess  to produc e clean-up and 
in-between drawings  
The preliminaries of this specialization course include the following: 1) discussion on the relevance of the course; 2) expla nation of key con cepts of common 
competencies; 3) explanation of core competencies relative to the course; and 4) exploration o f career opportunities.

Course Description:  
This is a specialization course which leads to an Animation  National Certificate Level II (NC II). It covers Personal Entrepreneurial Competencies (PECs); 
Environment and Market (EM); and one (1) Core Competenc y that a high school student ought to possess to  produce clean -up and in -between drawings.  
The preliminaries of this specialization course include the following: 1) discussion on the relevance of the course; 2) expla nation of the core competencies relative 
to the course; and 3) exploration of career opportunities.
