Getting started

What does AlignED do?

It makes DepEd lesson plans in your school's own format. Pick a format, fill in a short setup (teacher, subject, course guide), then let AlignED write the plan or write it yourself field by field with Alan's help. The finished plan looks like your format and exports as a Word file.

What can students, teachers and admins each do?

Students make lesson plans and submit them to the class they joined. Teachers make lesson plans, create sections for their students, and review what students submit. Admins manage accounts, sections, documents and announcements for the whole school.

Can I see Alan's tour again?

Yes. Use Replay the tour at the top of this page. Alan opens each tab and points out what it is for.

Formats

What is a format?

It is the layout your plan is printed in, such as the DLP, ILAW, 4A's, PAL3 or SNHS DLP. Every plan follows its format exactly, from the header table to the procedure.

Why can't I edit the ready-to-use formats?

They are set up for everyone, so they stay as they are. To change one, make your own copy: go to Create Lesson Plan and use New format.

How do I add my school's own format?

In Create Lesson Plan, choose New format and upload a blank copy of it as a .docx, .dotx or .pdf. AlignED shows a live copy you can tidy up (fix typos, delete leftover lines), and works out the fillable fields when you save.

AlignED says it couldn't read my file. What now?

That usually means the PDF is a scan (a picture of a page) rather than real text. Upload a .docx copy of the format, or export the PDF again from Word.

Making a plan

What goes in the setup step?

Who is teaching, the subject, the level, grade and section, and the course guide the lesson comes from. The lesson is written from that guide, so pick the one that matches your class.

Building Proficiency or Guided mode: which should I use?

Building Proficiency writes the whole plan at once, and you review it after. Guided mode takes you through the fields that need real thinking one at a time; you write each one and can ask Alan in the chat for help.

What is the "Also write these for me?" list?

The quick fields are filled in automatically. The list shows the bigger ones (objectives, procedures, assessment and so on). Untick any you want to write yourself and AlignED leaves them for you.

Why are Lesson Title and Session No. left blank?

They are yours to fill in. If you would rather AlignED wrote them, tick them in the list, or use Generate these too after the plan is written.

ILAW has a column for each day. How is that filled?

Each of those rows gets one answer per day, Monday to Friday, each in its own cell, so the week builds day by day.

The document looks small. Can I make it bigger?

The page is shown at its real size, fitted to your screen (Fit). Use the zoom buttons in the toolbar to make it bigger, and the zoom readout to go back to Fit.

A field came back empty after generating. What do I do?

Write it in yourself, ask Alan about it in the chat, or generate the plan again.

Course guides and subjects

Which subjects does AlignED support?

ICT, Computer Programming, AFA (Agri-Fishery Arts), HE (Home Economics, now called Family and Consumer Science or FCS), IA (Industrial Arts), and Electrical (Electrical and Electronics Servicing).

Where do the official guides come from?

From DepEd: the MATATAG EPP/TLE Curriculum Guide and the Three-Term Budgets of Work published on the DepEd LR Portal. They are marked as official and can't be changed.

Can I add my own course guide?

Yes. Open Curriculum/Course Guide and use Upload a guide. Everyone can then find it in the shared library.

Saving, drafts and exporting

Where are the plans I haven't finished?

In List of Lesson Plans. Drafts are kept there; use Continue to pick up where you left off.

How do I get a Word copy?

Save the plan, then use Export as Word. The file keeps your format's exact layout.

Sections and submissions

Teachers: how do students join my class?

Go to My Sections and use Create Section. Each section gets a join code; give it to your students. What they submit appears in Submissions.

Students: how do I join a class and submit a plan?

Open My Classes and enter the code your teacher gave you. Then, in List of Lesson Plans, use Submit to class on the plan you want to hand in.

Announcements and the tutorial video

Where do I find announcements?

New ones show at the top of your pages and in your notifications. All of them are in Announcements.

Where is the tutorial video?

When your admin posts one, it plays inside its announcement. Open Announcements, or use Watch the video on the notice at the top of the page.

Admins: how do I post a video?

In Announcements, create or edit an announcement and choose a video (MP4 or WebM, up to 500 MB). It uploads in small pieces with a progress bar, so keep the window open until it finishes.

Your account

How do I change my name, email or password?

Open Settings, from the menu or your picture in the top corner.

I can't sign in. What should I do?

Check your email and password. If it still fails, your account may have been archived; ask your school admin.
